  SakhiCare AI 

   AI-Powered Women's Health Education Assistant

  1. About the Project

SakhiCare AI is an AI-powered women's health education assistant designed to help women understand their symptoms, learn about Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), and access reliable, easy-to-understand health information.

The platform uses AI to provide multilingual educational guidance in English, Hindi, and Kannada, encouraging women to make informed decisions about their health and seek professional medical advice when necessary.

  2. Problem Statement

Many women lack access to accessible and understandable information about reproductive health conditions such as PCOS. Language barriers, limited awareness, and hesitation to discuss personal health concerns can prevent women from seeking timely medical guidance.

  3. Our Solution

SakhiCare AI provides a simple, user-friendly platform where users can describe their symptoms and receive AI-generated educational information, self-care suggestions, and guidance on when to consult a healthcare professional.

The platform promotes awareness and early health education without replacing professional medical diagnosis or treatment.

  4. Key Features

- AI-Powered Symptom Guidance: Provides educational information based on user-described symptoms.
- PCOS Awareness: Explains common symptoms, possible contributing factors, and general health information about PCOS.
- Multilingual Support: Offers an accessible experience in English, Hindi, and Kannada.
- Personalized Educational Guidance: Presents relevant health information and general self-care suggestions based on the user's input.
- Care Guidance: Helps users understand when self-care may be appropriate and when routine or urgent medical attention may be needed.
- Simple User Interface: Provides a clean, responsive, and easy-to-use experience.
- Printable Summary: Allows users to print or save their health information summary for future reference.

  5. Technology Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Python, Flask
- AI Integration: Gemini API
- Development Tools: Visual Studio Code, Google AI Studio

  6. How It Works

1. The user selects a preferred language.
2. The user enters symptoms or health-related concerns.
3. The frontend sends the input to the Flask backend.
4. The backend processes the request using the Gemini API.
5. The AI generates educational information, self-care suggestions, and appropriate care guidance.
6. The frontend displays the response in a clear, accessible format.

  8. Responsible AI and Privacy

SakhiCare AI is intended for health education and awareness only.

- It does not provide a medical diagnosis or replace a healthcare professional.
- AI-generated responses may be inaccurate and should be treated as general educational information.
- Users should seek qualified medical advice for personal health concerns.
- Urgent or severe symptoms should be evaluated by appropriate medical services.
- Sensitive health information should be handled carefully, and unnecessary storage of user inputs should be avoided.

  9. Future Enhancements

- Expand health education content to cover additional women's health conditions.
- Improve multilingual accuracy and accessibility.
- Add reliable medical resources reviewed by healthcare professionals.
- Enhance privacy safeguards and response quality.
- Improve the experience for mobile and low-bandwidth users.

  10. Project Impact

SakhiCare AI aims to make women's health education more accessible, inclusive, and understandable by combining artificial intelligence with multilingual support and responsible health guidance.

  11. Disclaimer

SakhiCare AI is an educational tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical concerns.
